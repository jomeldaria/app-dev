# My Favorite Movie: *All of us are dead*
## Overview
**All of us are dead** is a South Korean zombie apocalypse series on **Netflix**.  
It follows a group of high school students who are trapped in their school during a sudden zombie outbreak.
### Main Cast
- Park Ji-hu as *Nam On-jo*  
- Yoon Chan-young as *Lee Cheong-san*  
- Cho Yi-hyun as *Choi Nam-ra*  
- Lomon as *Lee Su-hyeok*
